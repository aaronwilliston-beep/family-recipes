Chicken Quesadillas

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Season the chicken pieces with salt, pepper and the spices. Cutting the chicken up before cooking means every bite gets seasoning, not just the outside of a whole breast.
  2. Heat the olive oil and butter in a skillet. Cook the chicken over medium heat 7–8 minutes, until cooked through. Move to a plate to cool slightly.
  3. Heat a grill pan or skillet over medium-high.
  4. Brush one side of a tortilla with vegetable oil. With the oiled side facing down, put cheese on half, add chicken, then add a little more cheese on top to hold it together. Fold it over.
  5. Cook oiled side down 2–3 minutes, pressing lightly, until browned and the cheese has melted. Flip and repeat.
  6. Cut into thirds and serve with pico de gallo or guacamole.
