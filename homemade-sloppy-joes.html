The Best Homemade Sloppy Joes

Serves 6Prep 5 minCook 20 minTotal 25 min

Ingredients

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Melt the butter with the oil in a large skillet over medium to medium-high heat.
  2. Brown the beef, breaking it up, about 5 minutes. Drain it in a colander.
  3. In the same skillet, soften the onion and bell pepper 2–3 minutes. Add the garlic for about 30 seconds.
  4. Return the beef and stir in the tomato paste.
  5. Stir in the ketchup, water, brown sugar, mustard, chili powder, Worcestershire, salt, pepper, and the optional pepper flakes and hot sauce.
  6. Simmer over medium heat 10–15 minutes, until as thick as you like.
  7. Serve on toasted buns.

Family history

  • The family has made this one.

Notes

  • The cooled meat mixture freezes up to 4 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator and reheat.
  • About 266 calories per serving, per the source.
