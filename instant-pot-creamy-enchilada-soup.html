Instant Pot Creamy Chicken Enchilada Soup

Serves 8Prep 10 minCook 20 min pressureTotal about 30–40 min (plus time to come to pressure)

Ingredients

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

Instant Pot

  1. Put the chicken in the pot. Add the garlic, chili powder, Worcestershire, hot sauce, onion, bell pepper, beans, corn with its liquid, enchilada sauce, broth, salt and pepper.
  2. Seal and cook on Manual/High for 20 minutes. Quick release.
  3. Shred the chicken in the pot with two forks.
  4. Stir in the cream and sour cream, then the cheddar, until melted and smooth.
  5. Top with cheese, tortilla strips and cilantro.

Slow Cooker

  1. Grease the crock. Add everything except the milk, sour cream, cheese and tortilla strips.
  2. Cook on low 6–8 hours, until the chicken is cooked through.
  3. Take out and shred the chicken. Stir the milk, sour cream and cheese into the pot, then return the chicken.
  4. Cook on low another 15–20 minutes, until the cheese has melted and the soup has thickened.

Notes

  • The video and the printed recipe differ on hot sauce (Tabasco vs. Sriracha) and enchilada sauce amount (2 cups vs. 20 oz). Both work.
