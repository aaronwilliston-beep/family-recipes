Papa Daddio's Spaghetti

Serves about 6Prep 10 minCook about 2 hr simmerTotal about 2 hr 10 min

Ingredients

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Press the garlic. Heat the olive oil in a skillet and cook the garlic until you can smell it.
  2. Add the browned ground beef, sauce, tomato sauce and tomato paste. Swirl the water in the cans and add it.
  3. Sprinkle in oregano and Italian seasoning to taste. Stir in the sugar.
  4. Simmer two hours, or until it's time to eat.
  5. Cook the spaghetti according to the package and serve the sauce over it.

Family history

  • First made at the 2001 Hobson Beach reunion.
  • Papa Daddio is what Aaron goes by when he's cooking an Italian meal.
  • A regular in the household, and often requested whenever the family gets together.

Notes

  • Any normal-size jar of Prego works, and so does any other jarred sauce.
  • Serving count is a rough estimate (one package of noodles usually feeds about 6).
