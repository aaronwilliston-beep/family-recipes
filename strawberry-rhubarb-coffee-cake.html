Strawberry-Rhubarb Coffee Cake

Serves 15Prep about 45 min (fruit sauce, topping, batter)Cook about 1 hrTotal about 2 hr including cooling

Ingredients

Cake and pan

Fruit sauce

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Brush a 9x12x3-inch baking pan with softened butter and set it aside.
  2. Fruit sauce: in a medium saucepan, combine the lemon juice, cornstarch and 1 cup sugar. Add the strawberries and rhubarb. Cook over medium heat, stirring often, until the rhubarb is soft and the liquid has thickened, 15–20 minutes. Move to a medium bowl to cool.
  3. Crumb topping: combine 3/4 cup sugar and 3/4 cup flour in a medium bowl. Melt 1/4 cup butter over low heat, drizzle it over the flour mixture, and mix with your hands until crumbly. Set aside.
  4. Batter: whisk the remaining 3 cups flour and 1 cup sugar with the baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl. Cut in the remaining 1 cup chilled butter with a pastry knife or two forks until it looks like coarse meal.
  5. In a separate bowl, mix the eggs, buttermilk and vanilla. Pour into the flour mixture and stir just to combine.
  6. Spread half the batter in the pan. Spoon half the fruit sauce over it.
  7. Carefully spread the rest of the batter over the fruit, then top with the rest of the fruit sauce.
  8. Sprinkle the crumb topping over everything.
  9. Bake about 1 hour, until golden brown and the center springs back when touched.
  10. Cool slightly on a rack. Serve warm or at room temperature, cut into squares.

Family history

  • Guenevere makes this one. If it stays in the house it's gone in a day or less, so a lot of it goes out to friends.

Notes

  • The batter is like a biscuit dough: the butter is cut in cold rather than creamed, which gives a tender crumb.
