Instant Pot Blue Ribbon Chili

Serves 6Prep 5 minCook 28 min (plus up to 15 min to reach pressure)Total about 45 min–1 hr (includes 10–15 min cooling)

Ingredients

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Set to Sauté (High) and melt the butter with the oil. Add the onion, poblano, jalapeño and habanero and cook about 5 minutes, until soft. Add the garlic for 1 more minute. Take out and discard the habanero.
  2. Add the beef and cook about 3 minutes, just until crumbled and lightly browned. Don't drain.
  3. Stir in the beer, diced tomatoes, Rotel, tomato sauce, taco sauce, Worcestershire and hoisin, scraping the bottom of the pot well.
  4. Stir in all the dry seasonings.
  5. Spread the beans on top. Don't stir them in.
  6. Seal and pressure cook on High for 5 minutes. Quick release, then leave it on Keep Warm.
  7. Stir well, then stir in the chili base. Let it cool 10–15 minutes before serving.

Family history

  • A regular in the house when the weather turns cold.
  • Took 2nd place at the 21 SOPS chili cook-off in 2022. It wasn't a serious competition, and the tasters arguably didn't get enough chili to make a good call. Carlos won, but he put a lot of sugar in his.

Notes

  • Salt: several commenters found it salty with 4 tbsp of the chili base and cut it to 2–3 tbsp. No-salt-added tomatoes and low-sodium Worcestershire also help.
  • The chili base is optional but adds a lot of flavor. It's easier to find online than in stores.
  • Doubling works in an 8-quart pot, not a 6-quart.
  • Burn warning: layering the beans on top and scraping the bottom well after the liquids prevents it.
  • Optional: roast the poblanos at 450°F for 5–10 minutes with oil and salt first for deeper flavor.
  • Tastes better the next day and freezes well.
