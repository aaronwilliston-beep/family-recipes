Guenevere's Cinnamon Rolls

Serves several pansPrep about 45 minCook about 20 minTotal about 3 hr (includes two rises plus a short third rise)

Filling

Icing

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Sift the 8 cups flour into a bowl. Cut the 2 sticks of room-temperature butter into small cubes.
  2. Yeast: stir the sugar into the warm water, add the yeast, and stir. Let it sit about 10 minutes.
  3. In a big bowl or stand mixer, mix the cold milk and vanilla pudding.
  4. Add the cubed butter, eggs and salt to the pudding and mix. Add the yeast mixture and mix.
  5. If using a stand mixer, switch to the dough hook. Add the flour 1 cup at a time, mixing in each cup. After the 8th cup, knead by hand or mix on medium until the dough clears the sides of the bowl, about 3–4 minutes.
  6. Form the dough into a ball. Coat the inside of a large bowl with olive oil, put the dough in, cover, and let it rise 1 hour or until doubled.
  7. Punch down, cover, and let it rise again for 30 minutes.
  8. Butter the pans: a 9x13 plus whatever smaller pans you have. One batch fills more than one 9x13.
  9. Sift the brown sugar and cinnamon together in a separate bowl.
  10. Turn the dough onto a floured surface and knead just to remove the extra air. Roll it out with a floured rolling pin to about 34 x 21 inches.
  11. Brush with the melted butter. Sprinkle the brown sugar and cinnamon all the way to the edges, then roll it up the long way.
  12. Cut into 2-inch rolls (or any size you like) using floss.
  13. Place the rolls in the pans with space between them so they have room to expand. Cover and let rise another 20 minutes (longer if frozen).
  14. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  15. Icing: sift the powdered sugar into a bowl. In a separate bowl, beat the butter, cream cheese and vanilla until fluffy, then slowly mix in the powdered sugar. Add milk as needed to thin it.
  16. Uncover the rolls and bake 10 minutes, rotate the pans, and bake about 10 minutes more, until lightly brown.
  17. Slather on icing if you like.

Family history

  • Guenevere likes to make these and give them out to friends, because if she leaves them in the house they're gone in a day or less.

Notes

  • To freeze: after cutting, set the rolls on a pan and freeze just until hard, then store in a gallon Ziploc bag and take out as needed. Let them thaw and rise in a warm place, then bake as usual.
  • Cutting with unflavored floss gives clean rolls without squashing them.
