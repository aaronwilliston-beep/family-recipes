Slow Cooker Chicken Taco Bowls (High Protein Meal Prep)

Serves 10Prep 15 minCook 2–3 hr high or 3–4 hr lowTotal 2.5–4.5 hr

Ingredients

Taco Chicken

Bone Broth Rice

Chipotle Lime Sauce

To Finish

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Put the chicken, taco seasoning, enchilada sauce, corn, beans and salt in the slow cooker. Cook on high 2–3 hours or low 3–4 hours, until tender and cooked through.
  2. Near the end, bring the rice and bone broth to a boil in a pot. Cover, reduce to low and simmer about 20 minutes. Rest 5 minutes covered, then fluff.
  3. Shred the chicken in the slow cooker. Stir in the cooked rice and a handful of cilantro.
  4. Blend all the sauce ingredients until smooth, adjusting seasoning and thickness with milk.
  5. Divide into 10 containers, drizzle with sauce, and garnish with cilantro.

Notes

  • Per serving, as stated by the source: 535 cal, 44 g protein, 64 g carbs, 10 g fat.
  • Storage: freeze for best results. To reheat, cover with a paper towel and microwave 3–4 min, stir (add a splash of water or broth if dry), then 1–2 min more.
