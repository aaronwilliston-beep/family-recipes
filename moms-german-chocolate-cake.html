Mom's German Chocolate Cake

Serves one 3-layer 9-inch cakePrep about 45 minCook about 30 min bake + about 12 min for the frostingTotal about 2 hr including cooling

Ingredients

Cake

Coconut-Pecan Frosting

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

Cake

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line the bottoms of three 9-inch round pans with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the chocolate with the water in a small saucepan (or in the microwave). Let it cool.
  3. Separate the eggs. Beat the 4 whites until stiff peaks form, then put them in the refrigerator until needed.
  4. Mix the flour with the baking soda and salt and set aside.
  5. Cream the butter, then beat in the sugar until light and fluffy.
  6. Beat in the egg yolks one at a time, then the melted chocolate and vanilla.
  7. Add the flour mixture alternately with the buttermilk, mixing just until smooth after each addition.
  8. Fold in the beaten egg whites by hand, about a third at a time, lifting from the bottom. Don't use the mixer for this; folding keeps the air in so the cake is lighter.
  9. Divide the batter among the pans. Bake about 30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  10. Cool in the pans about 15 minutes. Run a knife around the sides (the parchment lets the bottom pop out), turn out, and cool completely.

Frosting

  1. In a saucepan, stir the evaporated milk into the egg yolks a little at a time so the yolks don't scramble. Add the sugar, butter and vanilla.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring, about 12 minutes, until thick, bubbly and golden brown.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the coconut and pecans. Cool until thick enough to spread (the refrigerator or freezer speeds this up).
  4. Spread frosting on top of each layer and stack. Traditionally the sides are left unfrosted.

Family history

  • Mom has made this exact version specifically for Dad for as long as Aaron can remember, his whole life and longer.
  • She uses her "trusty old recipe," the original Baker's version with separately beaten egg whites. She says the newer package recipe dropped that step because nobody had the patience for it.
  • In the video she makes it for Aaron's 48th birthday, in Guen's kitchen on Guam, and films it as a full tutorial so the family can make it themselves.
  • "I never pretended to make cute, beautiful cakes, just delicious cakes."

Notes

  • What makes it Mom's: the egg whites are beaten separately and folded in, as in the original package recipe. Everything else is the classic package recipe.
  • Her layers were done after about 25 minutes, so it's worth checking a few minutes before 30.
