Supermarket-Style Sandwich Loaf Bread

Serves 1 loaf (9 x 4.5 in pan)Prep 20 minCook 35–40 minTotal about 3.5 hr (includes two rises and cooling)

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Warm the water and milk together to about 98°F (37°C). Stir in the yeast, cover lightly, and let it sit 10 minutes, until foamy.
  2. In the stand mixer bowl, whisk the flour, salt and sugar. Fit the dough hook.
  3. With the mixer on low, slowly pour in the yeast mixture. Mix until combined, scraping the sides as needed.
  4. Once it forms a cohesive dough, keep mixing 2–3 minutes, until smooth.
  5. Add the butter 1 tbsp at a time, letting each piece work in. Mix 30 seconds more after the last addition.
  6. Shape into a smooth ball, put it in a greased bowl, cover with greased plastic wrap, and let it rise at room temperature 1–1½ hours, until doubled.
  7. Grease the loaf pan with butter. Punch the dough down and roll it on a lightly floured surface into a rectangle about 1/2 inch thick and as wide as the pan.
  8. Roll it up tightly into a log and set it seam-side down in the pan. Cover with a damp towel or an upside-down loaf pan and let it rise 45–60 minutes, until doubled.
  9. Bake at 350°F for 35–40 minutes, until golden brown. Don't brush the top with anything.
  10. Turn out and cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.

Notes

  • Yeast: the video says 1 tbsp (9 g). The description says "1 packet? (9 grams?)". A standard packet is 7 g (2¼ tsp), so one packet is slightly less than he uses. Either works; 9 g rises a bit faster.
