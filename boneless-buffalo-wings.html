Boneless Buffalo Wings (Applebee's Copycat)

Serves about 2 lb chicken (serving = 10 wings)Prep 10 minCook 6 min fryingTotal about 7 hr (6 hr brine + 30 min freeze)

Ingredients

Chicken & Serving

Brine

Dry Mix

Wet Mix

Hot Sauce

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. Cut the chicken into about 1-inch cubes. Soak in the brine in the refrigerator for 6 hours.
  2. Set up two bowls: one with the combined dry mix, one with the whisked wet mix.
  3. Dredge the chicken in this order: flour, egg, flour, egg, flour (three flour coats). Lay the pieces on wax paper and freeze 30 minutes.
  4. Make the sauce: melt and combine the butter, hot sauce, Worcestershire, garlic powder and cayenne in a small pot. Stir in the cornstarch last.
  5. Make the dip: stir Gorgonzola chunks into the blue cheese dressing.
  6. Fry the chicken at 350°F for 4 minutes. Right before serving, fry again for 2 minutes.
  7. Toss in a large bowl with the hot sauce until coated. Serve with celery and the blue cheese dip.
