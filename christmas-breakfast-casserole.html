Christmas Breakfast Casserole

Serves 8Prep about 30 min (including browning the sausage)Cook 45 min+ (until 160°F in the center)Total about 1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. If using turkey sausage, brown it in a skillet first and drain it.
  2. Spray a 9x13 casserole pan with cooking spray. Break up the hash browns into the pan.
  3. Mix all the other ingredients together and pour over the hash browns. Top with a handful of shredded cheese.
  4. Bake at 350°F covered for about 40 minutes.
  5. Uncover and keep baking until a thermometer in the center reads 160°F. Check the very middle, because that's the last part to heat through.
  6. If baking straight from the refrigerator, it needs quite a bit longer. Go by the thermometer, not the clock.

Family history

  • A Christmas tradition. It's made almost every year.

Notes

  • The thermometer is required, not optional. Some of the ingredients go in frozen (the hash browns) and some don't, so the bake time is never the same twice. 160°F in the center means the eggs are done.
  • The "straight from the refrigerator" note means it can be assembled the night before.
