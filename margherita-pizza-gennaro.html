Classic Margherita Pizza (Gennaro's)

Serves 4 pizzasPrep 20 minTotal about 2.5 hr (includes 2 hr proof)

Steps

Tap a step to mark where you are.

  1. In a bowl, mix the flour and salt, then add the yeast. Pour in the warm water and stir with a spoon until it comes together.
  2. Turn the dough out onto a lightly floured surface. Knead by pulling and folding it over itself, working around the dough, until smooth.
  3. Cut into 4 equal portions and roll each into a ball.
  4. Set the balls on a tray dusted with semolina, dust the tops with flour, cover with a damp cloth, and proof at least 2 hours, until doubled.
  5. Press each ball out by hand into a round, pulling dough in from the edges toward the middle and pressing with your fingertips.
  6. Spread a thin layer of tomato from the center outward. Drizzle with olive oil and add a pinch of salt. Add Parmesan, torn mozzarella and basil, then another drizzle of oil.
  7. Slide onto a semolina-dusted peel and bake until the crust is blistered and the cheese has melted.

Notes

  • Needs info: oven temperature and bake time aren't given in the video. The usual home approach is the hottest your oven goes (500–550°F) on a preheated pizza stone or steel, about 7–10 minutes. Confirm against the written recipe on jamieoliver.com if you want his exact numbers.
  • Portion math: 500 g flour + 325 ml water ≈ 830 g of dough, so 4 balls are about 205 g each. He says "about 150 g" in the video, which doesn't match. 4 portions is what he actually cuts.
  • Other topping ideas from the video: salami, preserved vegetables.
